# Patient Analytics and Care Consistency Dashboard

Hospital patient-visit analysis — cost, length of stay, and readmission risk by department

**Live Dashboard:** https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/deep.kaur7386/viz/PatientAnalyticsandCareConsistencyDashboard/PatientAnalyticsCareConsistencyDashboard

## Overview
Analysis of 5,000 patient visit records across five hospital departments (Cardiology,
General Medicine, Neurology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics), covering visit volume, treatment
cost, length of stay, and readmission risk.

## Key Findings
- **Visit Volume:** Patient visits are evenly distributed across departments (967–1,058 
  visits each), indicating balanced demand with no single department as a bottleneck.
- **Treatment Cost:** Average treatment cost is consistent across departments 
  ($53,506–$55,762), suggesting standardized cost structures rather than outlier pricing 
  in any specialty.
- **Length of Stay:** Average length of stay holds steady at ~4.0–4.1 days regardless of 
  treatment type (Medication, Surgery, Observation, Therapy).
- **Readmission Risk:** Average readmission risk is nearly identical between Emergency 
  (0.28) and Routine (0.28) visits, showing visit urgency alone does not predict 
  readmission risk in this dataset.

## Tools Used
Excel (pivot tables, data exploration), Tableau Public (dashboard visualization)

Healthcare Patient Analytics Dataset (patient-level visit records)
